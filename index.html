Otago Land Finder

4. Show

Merge lands

Pick a land, then press Add to merge. Add all the lands you want to join. They must touch each other. Press Merge to make them one land. It keeps the first land's name and adds up the prices and areas.

#Land

Find land by drawing

Press Add points, then click the map. Change a number to move a point. Press Finish to find the land: 1 point finds one land, 2 points make a line, 3 or more make a shape. Press Download shape to save your drawing as a shapefile.

#LatLng

Find land from a shapefile

Pick a .zip that holds the .shp, .shx, .dbf and .prj files. Then press Find land.
